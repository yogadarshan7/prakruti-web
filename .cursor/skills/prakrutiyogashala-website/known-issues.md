# Known issues on the live site (audit Aug 2026)

Fix these when touching the relevant page. Do not reintroduce them.

## Brand and trust

- Name appears as Prakruti Yogashala, Prakruti Yogshala, and Prakrutiyogashala.
- Gallery title: “Dharamsala, India” — school is in Rishikesh.
- 300h copy mentions “Rishikesh and Dharamshala” without a confirmed second campus.
- Home/YTT page claims “#1 Yoga School in Rishikesh” with no source.
- “15+ years experience” vs about-us “three years in” building the school.
- Yoga Alliance: FAQ correctly limits certification to 200h and 300h; other blocks imply all courses certify teachers. 100h must stay CE / foundation-only.

## Contradictory product details

- Batch cap: 15 vs 12 vs “only 12 seats” vs “Only 6 Spots Left.”
- Home: 26 days, 25 nights, 2nd–28th monthly — does not match 100h (12 days).
- Two different daily schedules on the 100h page (table vs “How the Day Unfolds”).
- Meal names mix “3 vegetarian meals,” “sattvic,” “high tea,” Ayurvedic — fine if consistent; currently not.

## Copy quality

- Grammar: “faculty combines of 15+ teachers”; “Our teachers to walk alongside you”; “Meals Everyday”; missing commas (“Rishikesh the Yoga Capital”).
- Generic TTC filler on 200h/300h vs a sharp, specific 100h page. Bring the others up, do not dumb the 100h down.
- Homepage repeats uniqueness, inclusions, and CTAs.
- Urgency stack: “Limited Time Offer,” “Prices Increase Soon,” “Only 6 Spots” fights the calm Gurukul positioning.
- 300h and 200h share boilerplate (“The course is conducted In Prakruti Yogshala…”).

## About / teachers

- Specialty tags duplicated onto the wrong bios (e.g. Sadhu Shree Ji tagged Anatomy/Therapy; Bhagyashree and Abhishek tagged like Jitendra; Nikhil/Mahesh/Deepti share “Yoga Asana / Anatomy / Therapeutic Adaptation”; Vikram and Suraj inherit Ayurveda/Reiki chips).
- Vikram Ji and Yogi Suraj have titles/chips but little or no bio.

## Technical / UX

- `https://www.prakrutiyogashala.com/sitemap.xml` 404.
- Contact encoded by Anti-Spam (CleanTalk) — “Click to decode” is a bad enroll moment.
- Forms repeat on many pages (“Where should Darshan send your personalised course recommendation?”).
- robots.txt is default WordPress (`Disallow: /wp-admin/`).

## SEO

- Overlapping titles: home, `/yoga-teacher-training-in-rishikesh`, and 200h all target “best YTT in Rishikesh.”
- Inconsistent slug style: `100-hours-...` / `200-hours-...` vs `300-hour-...` (singular). Do not change slugs without a redirect plan.
